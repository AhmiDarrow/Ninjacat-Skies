# 0.10.5 — Wildlife Returns and Wild Herds Stay

Tribal Power 5.3.14 and Shamanic Mounts 0.1.5, with Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.20 and Chocobos Reborn 1.1.4 as before.
Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 105 mods. Clients and servers must update together.

**The March fills again**

- **Wildlife returns.** Animals used to arrive only when March land was first made; once a stretch was hunted, tamed or
  emptied, nothing came back. Now, about once a minute for each player in the March, a few animals of that land may
  wander into a quiet spot 32 to 96 blocks away until about six creatures share the area. They come from the land's
  own list, spirit mounts included, so explored land fills again slowly to roughly how it began.
- **Wild mounts stay.** Untamed spirit mounts vanished like monsters as soon as nobody stood near, which in the March
  meant before anyone could reach them. Wild herds now stay where they were born, like any other animal.

**Storage**

- **The tidy button sorts the Deep Cache.** Through the Deep Cache, the Wayfarer Satchel and the camp vault, the
  button lit up and clicked but sorted nothing. It sorts now, and never puts the satchel inside its own vault.
