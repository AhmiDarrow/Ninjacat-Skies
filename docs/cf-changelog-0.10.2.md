# 0.10.2 — Fair Odds, and crops that grow

Chocobos Reborn 1.1.1 and Tribal Power 5.3.11. Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 105 mods.

- **Riders bump everywhere.** Two ridden chocobos used to pass straight through each other outside a heat (a ridden
  bird is a vehicle, and the game never pushes a vehicle). The kart contact racers already had on the course now
  runs for any bird a player is riding: in the paddock, on the square, on the road.
- **Rook never pays more than the purse.** The most Rook takes on a pick is the heat's first-place purse divided by
  the odds, so the biggest payout equals what winning the heat pays: 3 GP on yourself in a Class C sprint, 1 GP on
  the field at six to one, up to the sixteen a hand can hold on a long Class S grand prix. Rook tells you the limit
  with the odds; a bet placed before the heat was known gets the difference back if it is over.
- **Duel pots stop at the course purse**, so the winner nets one purse and the loser is out one.
- **A lost bet pays nothing.** A heat that ran to a result settles every bet against its winner; only a heat nobody
  at all finished scratches. Before, spectators got their stakes back whenever no rider finished, even when a field
  bird had won.
- **The March crops grow on crop sticks.** Emberroot, Fen Rice, Frostberry, Glimmer Bean and Steppe Grain asked
  AgriCraft for soil almost nothing is, and a fresh seed had no tolerance, so on farmland (or most March soil) they
  sat at their first stage forever. Each now grows on farmland and on the March's grass, soil and moss from
  strength one.
- **Three times as many wild patches** of the March crops, in every chunk of their own country.

**Server owners:** unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again.
