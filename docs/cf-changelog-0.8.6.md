# 0.8.6 — Put it down somewhere

Tribal Power 3.7.1. Existing saves load as they are, no quest ids change, and nothing a
Clowder has earned is touched.

- **Three Tribal Power recipes could not be crafted.** When two crafting recipes want the
  same shape and the same ingredients, one of them simply stops existing — the game picks
  the other. The **Wall Shelf** was a row of three March planks, exactly like the **March
  Planks Slab**, so nobody could ever make one. The **Hush Plate** and the **Gate Sigil**
  were hiding their counterparts the same way. The shelf is now three March planks over a
  stick, the Hush Plate takes a piece of **wool**, and the **Loom Seal** is a blank seal
  and a **Loom Thread**, matching the other voice seals. The Drift Plate and the Gate Sigil
  craft exactly as before.
- **Camp furniture is worth placing now.** The **Wall Shelf** holds four things along its
  board, the **March Table** four on its top, and the **Spirit Urn** one in its mouth. Use
  one with something in hand to set that down where you clicked, empty-handed to take it
  back; what you leave out is drawn where you put it and falls with the piece if it breaks.
  A comparator reads how full any of them is. Hoppers and pipes still leave them alone —
  these are furniture, and the caches are for storing things.
- **Sit down.** The **March Stool** seats one player. Use it empty-handed to sit, sneak or
  stand to get up; the seat never outlives the sitter or the stool.
- **The Wind Charm rings** when you knock it, and catches the air by itself now and then,
  quietly enough that a camp full of them is still somewhere you want to stand.

**Server owners:** unchanged from 0.8.5 — the server zip leaves out the four client-only
mods (Sodium, Reese's, BadOptimizations, Dynamic FPS). Client installs get all 104 mods,
servers 100. Unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again.

Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.249 / Java 21.
