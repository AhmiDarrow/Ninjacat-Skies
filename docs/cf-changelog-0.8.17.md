# 0.8.17 — Back on track

Chocobos Reborn 1.0.19. Core stays 0.5.17 and Tribal Power stays 5.3.10. Existing saves load as they are,
no quest ids change, and the mod list is unchanged at 104.

- **A wide line keeps the lap.** Leaving the road no longer throws the lap away. Swing wide and come back on.
- **A shortcut you should not take sets you back.** Cut further than a wide line, stay off for four seconds,
  or go over the edge, and you are set down on the road where you left it and held for a second. You keep the lap.
- **Every shortcut is marked where the road forks.** A coloured gantry, banners for the breeds that can take
  it, and a stripe down the road. The HUD tells you on the way in whether your bird can take it straight.
- **Older birds join the current rules once.** One old promotion mark becomes 3 points, two become 6. A bird
  with no born stats rolls a bloodline from its grade. Training, wins, colour and grade stay.

**Server owners:** unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again. Install this pack
on the server and on every rider.
