# 0.10.8 — Flat Glass, Flute and Post, Every Other Heat

Tribal Power 5.3.17, Shamanic Mounts 0.1.9, and Chocobos Reborn 1.1.7. Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.21 stays as it is.
Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 105 mods. Clients and servers must update together.

**Tribal Power**

- **Horizontal quartz glass panes.** Any quartz glass pane, clear, dyed, or lit, crafts on its own into a flat sheet,
  and back again. Place it like a slab: a floor sheet on top of a block, a ceiling sheet under one.
- **Sheets join up** side by side into one surface, with a rim on the open edges. Lit sheets shine at full brightness.
- **Lit glass is clearer**, closer to clear quartz glass.

**Shamanic Mounts**

- **Mount Flute.** A gold ingot over a stick over leather calls up to eight of your tame mounts to your side from any
  distance and any dimension. They arrive on follow.
- **Mount Trading Post.** Ride a tame up and use the post to offer it; another rider who uses a post on their own mount
  swaps with you. Sneak to offer a gift instead, which a player on foot can take.
- **Bred stats and gallop.** Health, Speed, Jump, and Stamina run 1 to 100 and pass down from the stronger parent.
  A mount from before this update rides exactly as it did. Hold sprint while riding to gallop for as long as stamina
  lasts.
- **The herd book opens up.** The spoiler switch is gone and every chapter is readable, except the chimera, which joins
  the book once you breed one. The book is solid in your hand, and the stag on its cover has eyes.

**Chocobos Reborn**

- **Chocobo Whistle** (from 1.1.6). Gold nuggets and a feather call your own tame chocobos from any distance, up to
  eight.
- **The named rivals race about half the heats.** Ahmi and Risika in Class C, Teiyo and Jolo from Class B. Esther says
  when they are running, and Rook hands back a stake on a rival who sits the heat out.
