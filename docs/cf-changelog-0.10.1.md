# 0.10.1 — The Fray

The cut in the sky is real now, and it stands where the world was cut. Core 0.5.19. Existing saves load as they are;
no quest ids change. 105 mods.

- **The Fray, at Loom's End.** Walk the broken gate-path north of the Cut Loom to its end and look up: nine dark
  threads come up out of the bottom of the void, rise past the edge and fray two hundred blocks into the sky. It is
  drawn on the sky itself, so it reads from the Knot, the market and the lookout as well as from the edge, and by
  night a violet rim keeps it in view against the stars.
- **It thins as the server reweaves.** Every Strand any Clowder online seats gathers one thread back, outermost
  first, and the dark cut behind them narrows. Once every Clowder online has rewoven, only the spine is left and it
  turns to a single lit gold thread that breathes. Lint sifts down out of the cut at the edge; when the sky holds,
  lit motes climb the thread instead.
- **`/skybound fray`** tells you how much of the server's reweave is closed. Operators can hold the Fray at a value
  to look at it (`/skybound fray 0.5`, `/skybound fray 1`) and release it with `/skybound fray off`.
- The old dark particle trickle over the overworld Dock is gone; the Codex's Fray pages point at Loom's End.

**Server owners:** unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again. The Fray's place is the
server's `ninjacatskies-common.toml` (`loom.frayDimension`, `frayX/Y/Z`); the defaults put it at Loom's End.
