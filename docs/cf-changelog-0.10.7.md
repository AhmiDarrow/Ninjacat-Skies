# 0.10.7 — Stone and Lit Glass, Breaking a Bronco

Tribal Power 5.3.16 and Shamanic Mounts 0.1.8. Chocobos Reborn 1.1.5 and Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.21 stay as they are.
Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 105 mods. Clients and servers must update together.

**Tribal Power**

- **Pressure plates and buttons** for March stone, March cobble, moonstone, and moss agate, including the polished and
  brick forms. Two of that stone in a row make a plate that players and mobs can trigger. One stone makes a button that
  stays down for a second.
- **Chiseled bricks** for moonstone and moss agate. Stack two brick slabs, or cut them on the stonecutter. March stone
  already had chiseled bricks.
- **Cracked bricks.** Smelt March stone bricks, moonstone bricks, or moss agate bricks. The stonecutter cuts them too.
- **Lit quartz glass.** Echo Attune takes glowstone dust. Seat quartz glass, a block or a pane, with a Fire totem
  nearby. The colour stays. Lit glass dyes the same way as clear glass, and six lit blocks cut into lit panes.

**Shamanic Mounts**

- **Wild adults hunt** a player within 16 blocks until a vanilla golden apple calms them for two minutes. Only in that
  window does a Shamanic Saddle go on, and only then can you mount. A vanilla saddle does not fit. The diamond apple is
  still the breed item.
- **Thirty seconds of reins.** An arrow and a word ask for left, right, forward, or back. Press that key. The mount
  bucks the opposite way. Fifteen prompts. Four misses are allowed. Getting off fails the try and gives the saddle
  back. A hit does not fail it. After a fail that mount refuses another try for 10 seconds.
- **It holds the kick** against a tree, a wall, or a low ceiling and bucks in place. Suffocation during the trial does
  not land.
