# Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.9

**Thread comes in skeins and bolts, and Steward Caches are worth opening.** Existing saves load as they are; no ids change. This replaces 0.5.8, which had lower cache odds and was never in a pack.

**Thread Skeins and Thread Bolts**

- A **Thread Skein** is nine Frayed Thread wound together, and a **Thread Bolt** is nine skeins (81 Thread). Both craft in a full grid and unwind back into nine of the smaller piece at any time.
- They exist because a trade slot holds one stack. A stall price above 64 used to be cut to 64 without saying so, so the big Spark stall prices are now paid in skeins and bolts.

**Better Steward Caches**

- **More of each roll is worth having.** Small caches now roll copper, iron and Frayed Thread about one time in four. Mediums are about half ingots, gold, redstone, lapis, experience and Thread. Larges are about three in five, with a chance of diamonds, emeralds, ender pearls, obsidian or a Thread Skein.
- **A piece of a life.** Every cache also makes one rare draw of its own: a **Thread Shard** at 1.5% / 4% / 12% (small / medium / large), or a whole **Thread of Return** at 0.2% / 0.5% / 1.5%. Combining caches never lowers your odds.
- Still no machines, progression tokens or caches inside a cache.
