# 0.8.15 — Two Suns and Three Moons

Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.17, Tribal Power 5.3.10 and Chocobos Reborn 1.0.16. Existing saves load as they are,
no quest ids change, and the mod list is unchanged at 104.

- **A new Overworld sky.** Two opposing suns, the gold Hearth and the blue-white Lantern, rise on opposite
  horizons and pass side by side at noon. Five moons cross the night at their own distances: the yarn-wound
  Skein, the ringed Rime, the void-black Hollow, the crimson Cinder and the far Glint.
- **Short nights.** Under two suns, dusk to dawn takes half as long. Server owners can turn it off with
  `shortNights` in the Core config.
- **Three moons over the March.** A new sun and three moons for Tribal Power's March: the silver Pale, the jade
  Loom with glowing veins and the small, burning Ember. Both skies are also cheaper to draw than before.
- **The Spirit Anvil.** Mend Spiritgear with Manifested Ingots at an anvil that never chips, and store ingots
  as the new Block of Manifested Metal.
- **Totem binding works again.** Sneak-click a Resonance Totem holding Spiritgear or a Spirit Charm to give it
  the totem's voice.
- **Smoother chocobo races online.** Fairer race timing under lag, a steady countdown, boosts and dashes that
  count properly, and AI riders that no longer stall or cut corners. Install the new Chocobos Reborn on the
  server and on every rider.

**Server owners:** unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again.
