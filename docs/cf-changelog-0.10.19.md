# 0.10.19 — Said Once

Tribal Power 5.6.3, Shamanic Mounts 0.1.15, Chocobos Reborn 1.1.14 and Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.23: the tooltip fix and a polish pass across all four. Nothing new to build, nothing moved. Existing saves load as they are. 106 mods.

**Across the pack**

- **Every hint prints once.** Since 0.10.18 each hint line showed four times, once per owner mod. Each mod now speaks only for its own items, and the pack's own tooltip layer drops the 22 Tribal Power lines that repeated or contradicted the mod's hints; the nine that add pack-only facts stay.

**Fixes**

- Tribal Power: pouch pickups fly to you and count; mesh, font, station and resonator settings save the moment they change; lighter per-beat and per-frame work; translatable Ley sight HUD.
- Shamanic Mounts: no ghost rider after a sneak-held teleport; a saddle caught in a rein trial when the world closed is handed back on load; bag slots refuse items server-side when no bags are strapped on.
- Chocobos Reborn: plain-digit queue clock; no spurious stalled-frames log on rejoin; race data released when a singleplayer world closes.
- Ninjacat Skies Core: guardians keep their arena shape across a reload and the Cogwright shows its first tile; a Driftwrecks pillar hover crash; Loomframe progress no longer runs past full; Craftweave picks and Clowder Hall plans do less work.
