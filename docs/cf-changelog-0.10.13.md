# 0.10.13 — Filtered Relays and Only Followers

Tribal Power 5.3.19, Chocobos Reborn 1.1.12 and Shamanic Mounts 0.1.13. Everything else stays as it is in 0.10.12.
Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 106 mods.

**Tribal Power**

- **A filter on every relay plate.** Open a plate for a Rune slot and eight filter slots that hold a picture of an
  item, never the item itself. A blacklist holds the marked goods back; a whitelist sends only them. Plates start as an
  empty blacklist, so they move everything as before. Mark a bucket to filter a fluid.
- **Aim plates with the Lattice Tuner.** The Bond slot is gone. Bonded pairs in your world turn into tuner links the
  first time both plates are loaded, and the bond items pop out beside them.
- **Plate hitboxes fit.** A plate on the side of a machine no longer has its hitbox floating on the far side of its block.

**Chocobos Reborn**

- **The whistle calls only the birds following you.** Birds left on Stay or Wander stay where you put them.

**Shamanic Mounts**

- **The flute calls only the mounts following you.** Mounts left on Stay or Wander stay put, and stay on Stay or Wander.
