# Ninjacat Skies 0.7.1-alpha — the Nine Tribes, and the guardians finished

Tribal Power **3.0.0** comes into the pack with every one of its additions wired into the quests and the Codex, and the alpha gaps left open in 0.7.0 are closed. Companion mods move to **0.4.1**.

## Tribal Power 3.0 — the Nine Tribes
Tribal Weave (chapter 34) grows by 31 quests, every one reachable from a void pad because everything new stands in the March beyond the Gate Drum:
- **The March remembers.** Ancestor Halls (advancement), Loom Thread, Lore Tablets, the Silent Drum, The Unsung (advancement *The Drum Remembers*), the Unsung Heart and the **Resonance Totem (Loom)** — the sixth voice — plus the Loom Seal, the Echo Unweave and the Sixfold Staff's Tether and Stitch.
- **The Nine Tribes.** Offerings at a Tribe Hearth, Friend standing, the Tribe Mark from an Elder at Voice, the Kinship Totem (up to fifteen voices for the Pulse Resonator), and a camp Drummer feeding your Drumheart.
- **Rites.** All six Rite Tablets with their reagents and seals, and *First Rite*.
- **Bound spirits and shared camps.** Bonding Charm and *A friend in the dark*; Camp Charter and *One vault, one budget*.
- **Reading the lattice.** Ley Lens, Pulse Gauge, Pulse Threshold, and the Codex's diagnostic report.
- The Whisker Codex's nine tribe pages each gain their camp (where it stands, what its hearth favours, what its Elder trades), plus *The Nine Camps* and *The Unsung* entries and five new Living Lattice pages. Tooltips on the new items point at the right chapter.
- Every quest keeps its id: appended quests draw ids from a per-chapter block, so no chapter after Tribal Weave moves and no progress is lost.

## Snapped Guardians — the 0.7.0 gaps
- **Grindcore** now does what the design says, against the real Ex Deorum API: a wearer's hand sieves get one extra fortune roll (Ex Deorum's own 30 % odds, one more level). Mining Fatigue immunity stays; the Haste stand-in is gone.
- **Hivecall** hurries hives for real: every Productive Bees hive or nest and every vanilla hive within 16 blocks of the wearer gets one extra tick every ten — 10 % faster, through the mods' own tick methods.
- The Overweaver's **shades carry no boss bar**; only the loom itself does.
- **Totem recipes are gated.** They appear in the recipe book when the Strand is seated (Lint Golem with Soil, Tangle with Claw, the two insane ones after the Reweave), Clowder mates who were offline get theirs at login, and a gate totem only answers a Clowder that has seated that Strand — the tooltip says so.
- A new **Snapped Guardians** quest chapter (39, in the Reweave group): totem → re-tension → relic for all thirteen, the gate totems depending on each Strand's *Seat* quest, the sealed door on the Reweave. The Codex gains a **Snapped Guardians** category: the ritual, then one entry per guardian with its arena, its fight, its totem and its relic, unlocking as Strands seat.

## Bug sweep
Every line of the five companion mods, every KubeJS script and every quest/Codex/recipe file was reviewed, and the pack was run: the whole 93-mod pack booted as a dedicated server with its log audited, and — for the first time — a **real client** (headless, Xvfb + software GL, `tools/guardians_showcase.py`) joined a dev server, rendered all thirteen guardians, watched them move, and ran a complete Lint Golem arena (summon → arena dimension → win → advancement → relic → home). Fixed:

- **KubeJS (shipped broken since 0.6.6):** `voidloom_recipes.js` used an ES6 shorthand property (`{ type }`) that Rhino rejects — the whole script failed to load, so every Voidloom recipe it defines was missing in-game. Two `const`s inside a `try` block also made Rhino throw "redeclaration" on the second recipe, which silently disabled the sieve-mesh aliases (Voidloom thread meshes in Ex Deorum sieves). Both fixed; a new gate (`tools/gates/test_kubejs_rhino.py`) parses every script with the pack's own Rhino and flags `const` in loop/try blocks.
- **Quests:** chapter and milestone subtitles used the wrong lang key suffix (`subtitle` → `chapter_subtitle` / `quest_subtitle`) and never displayed.
- **Guardians, arenas:** the arena exporter mirrors Blender y into Minecraft −z, and five mechanics kept the un-mirrored angles — the Overweaver's shades spawned over the void (a free win), the Hivemind's smoke points were 30° from its cells (immune forever), the Sealbreaker's pillars, the Beddown's seams and the First Cut's seam line were all mirrored; fixed with the plans checked block-by-block. Also: the Unwoven's tile-pattern quote fired "wrong tile" every tick and could draw unsolvable sequences; Drumheart lava rose through the beat pads; the Unwoven and Edgewalker suffocated in their own scenery (guardians no longer take in-wall damage); the Unwoven's true-form threads stood on air and its heddle drop deleted whole rows; the Grindmaw counted the quarry's own gravel as jams; Thornmother stations were 6 blocks off; a mid-fight restart always wiped; a wipe skipped the boss's cleanup (dirt, comb, lava, shades stayed in the slot); a player could end up in two arena parties and be fenced to death; giant bosses' path-finding was capped; the arena dimension was midnight-dark (now noon under the End sky); `voidloom` is declared as a dependency.
- **Guardians, relics:** relic timers were saved against the per-session server tick counter, so after a restart every saved "until" sat hours in the future — Edgestep i-frames became permanent invulnerability, Reweave/Root/Shred/ward flags stuck, Edgestep charges froze (now the world's game time, expired flags dropped); two copies of one relic doubled its passives; the Shard of the First Cut's armour pierce never applied (registered too late in the damage pipeline); Reweave turned shared infinite effects into permanent ones; Downbeat's NoAI stun could stick to unloaded mobs; relic bees were orphaned as permanent hostile bees and poisoned players; `onWearerHit` counted arrows and relic lines as melee; Knotcharm rooted Guardians and pets; Bloom hedges were harvestable berry farms; static relic state leaked across worlds in single-player; the renderer lost sub-tick animation on old worlds (float precision).
- **Ninjacat Skies:** leaving a party revived an exhausted Clowder (exhausted players no longer bring fresh lives to any team); turning lives off stranded spectators; a solo player's Tension Post became claimable after a month offline; the Fragment on a second Post gave the wrong message; the cache game test could never pass.
- **Clowder Hall:** the ceremony chest refilled on every `/clowder hub` — an unlimited Frayed Thread faucet (now once per player); dimension travel while riding froze the player; an offline party owner could not be removed from a mirror party; op commands on the spawn team would mirror the whole Dock into one party; parties showed a sanitised short name; `/clowder accept` failed on a deleted team's stale invite; social settings were rewritten on every join; sleeping in the Hall set a respawn there; the spawn fallback edited Dock blocks; Charter spam re-sent prompts. **Voidloom:** the Spindle Hammer and Crook now carry the Ex Deorum hammer tag and the enchantable tags in the mod itself.
- **Pack data:** two Create Dragons Plus loot-table overrides conditioned on a mod that does not exist made both fragile tanks drop nothing; removed.

## Verified
