# Ninjacat Skies Core 0.6.1

**Bug sweep.** Existing saves load as they are.

**Driftwrecks**
- Dying in a rift no longer deletes your gear or hands it to a mate: your drops come back when you respawn. Rewards earned while you are down wait for you.
- A disconnect or server restart no longer forfeits a rift. The rift waits up to five minutes for a missing member.
- Only the Clowder a wreck drifted in for can open its chests, light its pillars or open its rift.
- Logging out on a wreck that unravels while you are away now brings you home instead of leaving you over the void.
- A disbanded party's wreck now unravels.
- Watchtower Holds no longer carry a real, mineable beacon.
- Atlas messages read correctly in every language, and long perk lines wrap.

**Snapped Guardians**
- Fixed a way to mine and keep arena blocks.
- Relogging or dying no longer resets relic cooldowns.
- The largest guardians now have their full intended health. Minecraft capped them at 1,024, so The First Cut, the Overweaver and large-party Sealbreaker fights are tougher than before.

**Craftweave**
- On the Tribal Bench, taking a result now gives the recipe you picked.
- Crafting a quantity never switches to a different recipe partway.
- Clearing or refilling the grid with a full inventory no longer throws items on the ground.
- Recipe cycling gets past two recipes that make the same item.
