# Ninjacat Skies 0.8.0 — Driftwrecks

Pack CurseForge client file **8922954**, server additional **8922958**.

Ships **Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.1**, which adds a sixth companion mod: **Driftwrecks**. Once your Clowder has tensioned Soil, pieces of the old world drift up to your pad now and then, hold for a while, and unravel back into the void. Repeatable side content: nothing on the main line needs it.

- **Arrivals.** Drift pressure fills with your Clowder's online time (about 90–120 minutes a wreck). A **Driftlure** hung on the Tension Post calls one early; a Strand Lure leans it toward that tribe. One wreck per Clowder, 160–320 blocks out from your Post.
- **Getting there.** The **Drift Needle** points at your wreck. A **Tether Spool** lays a walkable thread bridge across the void, climbing in half steps. Fall off and the thread catches you, for three hearts.
- **No two alike.** Every wreck is assembled when it arrives: a rock keel and deck in one of six shapes, the place's core (Shrine, Watchtower, Library, Forge, Garden or Vault; three variants per size, turned to any bearing), a scatter of shared ruins from a library of eighteen (arches, wells, stalls, towers, kilns, gazebos...), tethered islets on the bigger ones, and a frayed seam. Then it wears a tensioned Strand's palette and one of six modifiers (Unmarked, Overgrown, Frozen, Haunted, Burning, Unstable). Three sizes: Raft, Ruin, Hold. Each asks one thing: open the heart chest, break the frayed spawners, re-thread the pillars in the idol's order, walk a Steward echo home, or hold the seam through three waves.
- **Loot that's fair.** Every chest rolls separately for each player the first time they open it. When a wreck unravels, nobody falls, and whatever you left behind comes home in a **Salvage Bundle** (in your **Salvage Crate**, if one stands by the Post). Blocks you placed on a wreck stay.
- **Salvaged Weft** is the wreck-only currency, spent at the **Salvager's Frame** on spools, lures, needles, map scrolls, Weft Keys, tribe decor and Keepsake reprints. It is not Frayed Thread.
- **The Wreck Atlas.** Nine Strands by six places, filled as you finish wrecks, with a lore page in the Codex for each. Full columns, rows and stamps pay small permanent perks. Hint pages open hidden rooms.
- **Keepsakes.** 54 small placeable objects, one per tribe and place; six of a Strand earn its Tribe Banner. The Trophy Plinth shows a set.
- **Sealed rifts.** A Hold's tear opens with a Weft Key and pulls your Clowder into a small chamber with a **Remnant**, a frayed echo of that Strand's Guardian keeping one of its mechanics. Nine Remnants, nine stamps.
- **The Heartwreck.** Fill every page and the heart of the old world drifts in, once.
- New quest chapter **Driftwrecks**, a Codex category, and `/driftwreck` admin commands. Server owners: `driftwrecks-server.toml` (enable, pacing, distances, lifetime).

**Existing worlds:** any Clowder that has tensioned Soil starts building Drift pressure on first load; its first wreck arrives after roughly 90–120 minutes of play (or at once with a Driftlure). Nothing existing changes ids.

Also ships **Tribal Power 3.6.0 - The living March** (CurseForge project **1684851**, file **8922875**): the Gate Drum needs no Pulse, you play the Gate Rite on it instead (use it empty-handed, hit the beats on A, S, D, F; 60% opens the gate), plus 27 March structures, six new trees with wood sets, lakes and water life, companions you tame and breed like wolves, catalyst-gated Spiritgear ranks and a rewritten Spirit Codex. The pack's quests and Codex now describe the Gate Rite. And **Chocobos Reborn 1.0.5 - Follow, Stay, Wander** (CurseForge project **1699008**, file **8922192**): orders for tame birds on the equipment screen; only Follow birds come along to Whiskerwind.

**Existing worlds (Tribal Power):** the March regenerates once more on first load of 3.6.0; the old one is moved to `tribalpower_backups` inside the save, nothing is deleted. Set `retrogenMarch = false` in `tribalpower-common.toml` before loading to keep it.
