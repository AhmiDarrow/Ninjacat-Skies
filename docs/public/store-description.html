Ninjacat Skies is a void-island skyblock quest pack for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge.

The Loom of Worlds was cut — only frayed pads of earth remain. Take to the sky as a Skybound initiate, form a Clowder, and reweave reality one Strand at a time.

Follow the Whisker Codex through nine Strands: Soil, Stone, Sprout, Claw, Spark, Clock, Swarm, Sigil, and Spindle. Weave Tribal Power shamanic technomancy — Spirit Pulse, Song Bench Echo, Gate Drum into The March — into the Hum / Bind / Reweave braid. When a Strand is seated, its Snapped Guardian can be called.

Requirements

  • Minecraft: 1.21.1
  • Loader: NeoForge 21.1.249
  • Java: 21
  • RAM: 6 GB minimum · 8 GB recommended (Profile Options → Memory → Recommended by Author / Custom 8192 MB)
  • Playtime: ~40–60 hour Normal campaign

After Install

  1. Open the Ninjacat Skies profile → Profile Options.
  2. Set Memory to Recommended by Author (8 GB) or Custom 8192 MB. 4096 MB is not enough.
  3. Enable the ninjacat-skies-ui resource pack if the title screen looks vanilla.
  4. In-world: read How to Start. On Clowder Dock, right-click the Island Charter → Create Team → pick a pad (Ninjacat Pad / Dojo Cottage / Frayed Thread).

Grave (`) opens the live quest list. Right-click the Whisker Codex for the campaign book (The Work, The Cut, The Loom Braid, Nine Tribes, Snapped Guardians).

Features

  • Skyblock void islands — Clowder Dock spawn + Easy / Normal / Hard pads
  • Clowder Hall hub dimension (/clowder hub)
  • FTB Quests Loom Braid progression — 1,593 authored quests across 39 chapters
  • Tension Post: seat Strand tokens on your pad; each tribe chimes, the notches light, and the pad itself changes (hearth, footing, haste, luck)
  • The Fray: the cut in the sky, visible over the Dock, thinning as the server reweaves
  • Whisker Codex in-game book: The Work, The Cut, the Loom Braid, Nine Tribes, and Snapped Guardians — entries unlock as you play
  • Snapped Guardians: thirteen bosses in their own arenas. Craft a Frayed Totem after seating that Strand, pull the Clowder in, and bring home a Woven Relic
  • Skyblock-tuned: every mod bootstraps from a bare pad — bees, Mystical Agriculture essences, archwood and more come from the sieve, no worldgen required
  • Bees on a void pad: craft a nest from a ring of logs or grit around a flower and wait for wings; every Productive Bees line starts there
  • Voidloom stations: a Loomframe that sifts on its own, a Tension Barrel that batches — the pad's first machines
  • FancyMenu branded title (void sky, paw logo, indigo/teal buttons)
  • Tribal Power 3.2.1 as a core pack pillar (also playable standalone)
  • Create, farming, bees, power, magic, and AE2 woven into original quests — not a generic kitchen-sink dump

Mods

Nearly 90 mods, all woven into the questline — Create, Mekanism, AE2, Powah, Mystical Agriculture, Productive Bees, Ars Nouveau, Occultism, Tribal Power, plus deep decoration (Chipped, Supplementaries, Handcrafted, Macaw's, FramedBlocks), storage, logistics, and quality-of-life (Xaero's maps, JEI/JER/JEP, Clumps). Companion systems ship as Ninjacat Skies Core (Voidloom, Clowder Hall, Whisker Codex, Guardians).

Multiplayer

Form a Clowder, claim an island together, and progress as a team. Shared pads and quest credit via Skyblock Builder + FTB Teams. Dedicated-server zips are uploaded beside the client pack.

Snapped Guardians

Thirteen keepers the Cut left feral — nine Strand guardians, two optional fights, and two insane stages after the Reweave. Craft a Frayed Totem, use it outside an arena, and you and nearby Clowder mates are pulled onto a dedicated stage. Win and every member present takes that guardian's Woven Relic: a trophy with a worn passive and a right-click power.

The Returning Song

Animated night auroras, a distinct March sky, three breedable animals and ten hostile spirits. Brush gentle fauna for renewable materials and recover guardian reagents for Echo processing. Overworld forests and swamps share species with the March. Client settings control sky brightness and detail.

A camp with a living heart

Tribal Power 3.2.1 brings an illustrated, animated Spirit Codex with search, bookmarks, live recipes and a spoiler warning. Nine tribe camps still stand: raise standing at a Tribe Hearth, trade with Elders, and wake The Unsung. Placement is the ritual — Stone Fonts, the Listening Pit, tablet Rite Circles, and built Way and Far Gates — with six voice generators around the Drumheart. Camp quests introduce ritual mob binding, finite-use summoning, chunk anchors, spawn wards, automated crop tending and functional decorations.
