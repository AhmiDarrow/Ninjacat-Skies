# 0.8.11 — Piled stones

Tribal Power 5.3.2. Existing saves load as they are, no quest ids change, and the mod list is
unchanged at 104.

- **Pulse Cairns pile up.** Cairns that touch on any side are one store: two hold 8,000, three
  12,000, up to 64 stones and 256,000 Pulse. Any stone reads, fills and drains the whole pile, and a
  bigger pile fills faster. Break a stone and it takes only its own share. The five-stone limit is
  gone.
- **A pile is one stone.** Touching cairns lose their seams and read as one rune-carved block, banded
  only round its outer edges, and the runes glow brighter and give off more light as the pile fills.
- **No more holes in Tribal blocks.** Looking into the Ley Collector or Stone Font, or up under an
  Ancestral Cache, no longer shows the sky through them; the same gaps on a dozen other blocks are closed.
- **A Pulse Cell fills in one use.** Right-click a Drumheart, Resonator, generator or cairn pile and
  the cell fills as far as it has room, not 25 at a time.
- **AgriCraft fixes.**
  - Vanilla flowers go on crop sticks: a poppy, tulip, dandelion, orchid, allium, bluet, daisy,
    cornflower, lily of the valley or wither rose now plants as its AgriCraft crop, and they grow on
    farmland as well as podzol.
  - Mushrooms, crimson fungus, nether wart and sweet berries plant the vanilla way again; AgriCraft
    had been turning them into crops that could never grow where you put them.
  - The March crops (Emberroot, Fen Rice, Frostberry, Glimmer Bean, Steppe Grain) and March soils
    are finally known to AgriCraft.
- **The Gear screen is readable** and the Tribal Power key names fit the Controls list.

**Server owners:** nothing changes for you. Unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run
`install` again.

Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.249 / Java 21.
