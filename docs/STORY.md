# Ninjacat Skies — Story Bible

## Logline

The Loom of Worlds was cut. The Skybound reweave it—one Strand at a time—from nothing but grit, green, and stubborn craft.

## Tone

Wry, tactile, lightly melancholy. Repair work in the clouds. Humor through naming and asides—not memes, not prophecy dumps.

## Canon (public-safe)

The skies were held by the **Loom of Worlds**, a lattice of living thread tended by the **Ninjacats**. The Ninjacats stood as **nine tribes**, each guarding one of the nine **Strands**. From their shared power—drawn through the Loom—**Tribal Power** was born: shamanic technomancy of pulse, lattice, seal, and the path into distant March-lands. Something severed the Loom. Continents fell. The tribes scattered. Pads of earth remain. Nine Strands still answer if pulled correctly. Orphan engines of Tribal craft still hum where stewards once worked.

Players are **Skybound**. Teams are **Clowders**. The **Whisker Codex** assigns work. Completing Strands restores **Loom Tension**—felt as world flair and late unlocks, not a second XP bar that nags. Learning Tribal systems is learning how the tribes once hummed the Loom.

## Design note — past the classic island ladder

Familiar void-island beats (sieve, farm, power, digital endgame) are **starting grammar**, not the campaign’s identity. Ninjacat Skies is meant to **evolve past** that classic flow into Loom-native systems: Strands that change how the pack behaves, parallel Clowder specialization, Voidloom and peer-mod arcs as equal routes, and a late game about **reweaving the sky**—not only maxing the usual tech checklist.

## Progression — The Loom Braid

Campaign shape is a **braid**, not a single tech ladder. Early phases reclaim a pad; mid-game opens three peer paths; late game binds steward craft and reweaves the cut.

| Phase | Strand | Focus |
|-------|--------|-------|
| **Wake** | Soil | Survive; Codex wakes; claim a pad |
| **Recover** | Stone | Matter from grit; Voidloom yarn, knots, meshes |
| **Root** | Sprout | Living anchors; food as infrastructure |
| **Edge** | Claw | Kit and footholds; leave the pad safely |
| **Pattern** | Clock | Factory as repeating loom pattern (Create) |
| **Colony** | Swarm | Living industry (bees / deep crops) |
| **Hum** | Spark | Pulse, heat, and rhythm—Tribal Power as primary hum; other FE as bridges |
| **Bind** | Sigil | Seals and rites with leftover steward tricks; peer magic remains available |
| **Reweave** | Spindle | Stitch the cut—digital loom, trophy, March foothold |

Pattern, Colony, and Hum are **peers**. A Clowder need not climb all three in lockstep before Bind and Reweave.

## The Nine Strands (40–60 hour spine)

Target total playtime: **40–60 hours** for a first clear on Normal, including automation and exploration side paths. Quest density **1322** FTB Quests across **34** chapters (main Strands, side boards, and the Frayed Thread Desk).

| h (approx) | Strand | Braid phase | Focus | Hours |
|------------|--------|-------------|-------|-------|
| 0–4 | Soil | Wake | Island, wood, water, sapling, Codex | 3–5 |
| 4–10 | Stone | Recover | Hammers, sieves, Voidloom yarn, ores, cobble gen | 5–7 |
| 10–18 | Sprout | Root | Food, Mystical T1–T3, irrigation, early auto harvest | 6–8 |
| 18–26 | Claw | Edge | Silent Gear kit, armor, nether foothold | 6–8 |
| 26–42 | Clock / Swarm / Spark | Pattern / Colony / Hum | Parallel specialization—Create, bees/crops, pulse/power | 12–16 |
| 42–54 | Sigil | Bind | Tribal rites & seals; Nature's Aura / Ars / Occultism peers | 5–7 |
| 54–60 | Spindle | Reweave | AE2 autocrafting, March foothold, Loom fragment trophy | 5–8 |

Side content (Clowder Hall bounties, dimension footholds, shop unlocks) fills gaps so the path does not feel linear railroad.

## Story devices

- **Whisker Codex** — quest entry + journal pages (short; one screen)
- **Frayed Thread** — currency; Frayed Thread Desk + craft sinks for QoL and alternate mats
- **Loom Tension** — per-player craft milestones (Strand tokens / braid / Spindle) with a small Thread soft unlock; hub plaque flair is optional later
- **Steward lines** — 2 Codex whispers on first Strand token (tribe-flavored); Codex Page every 3rd unique token; hub statue/NPC may echo the same voice—never walls of text
- **Tribal Power** — orphan technomancy of the nine tribes; core to Hum, Bind, and Reweave without replacing early Voidloom recovery

## Writing rules

- Prefer concrete verbs: sift, tension, splice, knot, reweave, hum, bind
- Quest titles ≤ 6 words when possible
- No “chosen one”; no unexplained ancient evil essay
- No references to other modpacks or their authors
- No anime or licensed-media taglines; shamanic technomancy voice only
- All lore and quest prose must be original — never paste another author’s book text
