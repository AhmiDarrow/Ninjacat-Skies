# 0.10.3 — The Full Bestiary, Chicobos and a Steady Herd

Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.20, Tribal Power 5.3.12, Chocobos Reborn 1.1.2 and Shamanic Mounts 0.1.4. Existing saves load
as they are; no quest ids change. 105 mods. Clients and servers must update together.

**Guardians and wrecks**

- **Guardian music plays.** Each guardian's own track takes over while you are in its fight and stops when it ends.
- **The Hivemind's drones fly.** They were spawning inside the amber wall and suffocating; they now come out in open
  air, so the fight is as hard as it was built to be. Bee relics no longer calm bees inside a guardian arena, and
  Drumpulse no longer stuns guardians or other bosses.
- **Fairer fights.** The Cogwright never repeats a tile, a restart no longer hands out a free damage window at the
  Overweaver or leaves the Beddown flooded, and Grindmaw grit no longer deletes blocks.
- **Drift wrecks.** Heartwrecks always come back, burning wrecks can no longer spread fire, the crumble catches
  overhangs, wreck placement no longer generates dozens of chunks in one tick, and the Atlas spells its stamps out.
- **Tribal guardians call their adds again**, and the adds hunt you. Bonded adds stay yours.

**Tribal Power**

- **Sixty-six creatures get Codex pages**, the Colossus Warden gets its own name, and the Shallows are described.
- **Glimmer Storms dull the Drumheart**, spirit leggings really shed cobwebs, berry bushes and soul sand, Loom boots
  stitch, and drum duels can no longer be won with a faked score.
- **No wool from nothing** (unweaving Spiritweave gives one wool back), and no boss in the Summoning Cradle.

**Chocobos and mounts**

- **Babies are chicobos**, everywhere. A Green and a Blue fed a Carob need their wins for a White too, as the almanac
  says. The duel picker only offers stakes the course can hold.
- **Shamanic Mounts:** every pelt flies in its own wing colours, mounts no longer flicker as they breathe, folded
  wings close, and riding gifts hold steady (night vision stays bright).

**Quests, the Codex and the hub**

- **Quests you could not finish on a void pad now can.** The pack adds recipes for the Shulker Shell, the Totem of
  Undying, the Ruined Book and the Magehunter, so the Crafting Proxy, Distributor, Archmage and Netherite spell books
  and the quests after them are reachable. About a hundred "How:" lines that named the wrong recipe are fixed, and
  every Tribal Power and Solar Flux quest names its real recipe.
- **Loom's End.** The Purring Hearth's keeper greets you as the hearth, not as the Seal-carvers; the Grit-singers and
  Drumhearts keepers carry their tribe names; keepers say their stalls are in Loom's End. The Codex no longer opens
  twice.
- **The Codex and tooltips** match the mods: the Fray stands at Loom's End, and the Harmonic Energizer, cairns, Shard
  Lamp, Stone Font and Resonator numbers are right.

**Server owners:** unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again.
