# 0.12.1 — Held Thread

A bug sweep and a translation pass. 106 mods. Existing saves load as they are.

**Fixes (Ninjacat Skies Core 0.6.1)**
- Dying in a Driftwreck rift no longer deletes your gear or hands it to a mate.
- A disconnect or restart no longer forfeits a rift.
- Other Clowders can no longer loot your wreck, work its pillars or take its rift.
- Logging out on a wreck that unravels no longer leaves you over the void. A disbanded party's wreck now goes away.
- Watchtower Holds no longer carry a mineable beacon.
- Guardian arena blocks can no longer be mined and kept, and relogging no longer resets relic cooldowns.
- The largest guardians now have their full intended health (Minecraft capped them at 1,024), so those fights are tougher than before.
- The Tribal Bench gives the recipe you picked. Quantity crafting keeps to one recipe, and a full inventory no longer throws the grid on the ground.

**Translations**
- **Tribal Power 6.2.0.** Its items, blocks and Spirit Codex are now translated too.
- The first-join How to Start book and its chat tips are no longer English only.
- Quest titles that name an item now match what the item is called in your inventory. Where a mod has no translation, the quest shows the English item name.
- Fixed broken sentences, mismatched item names, a few stray characters and one untranslated quest line.
