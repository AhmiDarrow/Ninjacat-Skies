# 0.11.1 — Gifts of the Knot

Tribal Power 6.1.0, real rewards for the Strand Knots, and the Totem of Undying goes back to being rare. 106 mods. Existing saves load as they are.

**Strand Knot gifts**

- **The Knot reward bag was empty.** Claiming a Strand Knot now opens a choice of gifts from that tribe, and every option shows what it is.
- **Pick one:** a **Thread of Return** for one more shared life, four rolls of the tribe's provisions, or a big pile of supplies: Steward Caches, Thread Skeins or Bolts, and diamonds, netherite, Totems of Undying or Nether Stars by how far along the Strand the Knot sits. One choice per team.
- **The Reweave** offers the same kind of gifts, without a Thread, since it already gives a life.

**Totem of Undying**

- **No longer craftable.** Totems now come only as quest rewards: Seat Clock, Seat Sigil, the late Strand Knots and a few side quests. The optional quests that ask for one say where to find it.

**Tribal Power 6.1.0**

- **Stairs, slabs and walls** for Pale Stone, Frost Shale, Ochre Sandstone, Salt Crust and the four Kiln Clays.
- **Every building block can be made**, Moonstone and Moss Agate bricks and polished stone included, and Salt Crust stacks into Salt Pillars.
- **The March's sands are sand**: they smelt to glass and make sandstone, TNT and concrete powder.
- **Spiritgear shears are shears**: Ribbon Weed, Veil Lichen, Echo Roots, Willow Strands and March leaves drop to them, they take Efficiency, Unbreaking and Mending, and dispensers shear with them.
- **Spiritweave waits on a totem**: unlinked pieces are plain armour that spend nothing, and a bound piece wakes its voice's boon and shines with it. Every piece's tooltip now names what each voice gives it.
- **Fixes**: the Healer's Rattle no longer charges mining Pulse or smelts drops; Fire smelting stays on the digging tools; the Hearth Pot's shift-click and any brush on a March creature work; saddlebags and pouches no longer spill when a creature is saved from death; guardians and The Unsung no longer call followers as they reset.
- **Faster**: floating islands and Weeping Colossi generate with far less work, ranged March spirits path-find less often, machines and hoppers re-read redstone less, and the sky, ley threads and creature models draw with less work per frame.
