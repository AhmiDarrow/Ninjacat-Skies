# 0.8.3 — Solid ground

Chocobos Reborn 1.0.8 (file 8926685). The race courses in Whiskerwind: a hole you could
fall through the world, a rider who could die in them, and twenty-four courses that now
treat every colour the same. Nothing else in the pack changes; existing saves load as
they are and no quest ids change.

- **You cannot fall out of the world on a course any more.** The course islands were
  stamped in steps along the centre line, and on a tight corner or a hill lip the steps
  skipped whole block columns: 761 holes clean through the twenty-four courses, 152 of
  them wide enough to swallow a bird. Falling in was not survivable either, because the
  rescue that puts a racer back on the road skipped anyone still over the road — exactly
  where the holes were. The islands are stamped four times finer, any gap under the
  racing area is filled, and a racer who ends up below the island is put back on the road.
- **Whiskerwind cannot hurt you.** The birds were already safe there; their riders were
  not, so riding a bird that cannot cross lava into a lava feature burned the rider to
  death and dropped their inventory into the void. A rider is now as safe as the bird, a
  burning one is put out, and anyone who walks off the edge is set down in the paddock.
- **A colour is worth the same on every course.** What your bird's ability saves you
  depended on where the feature happened to sit: on Starfall the water detour was
  actually *shorter* than the water, so a Blue bird lost time by using its ability, while
  going round Void's bog cost fifty blocks. Every feature has been re-placed on a bend so
  that going round costs about two per cent of a lap, on all twenty-four courses, and the
  bog is the cheapest thing to go round everywhere.
- **More boosts, in better places.** Three boost strips on a sprint and five on a grand
  prix, up from two or three, each on the exit of a corner onto a straight instead of
  mid-bend. Features are spread round the lap now, and the run off the grid is kept clear
  so the field is not hitting a detour while still three abreast.
- **Every course has its own landmark.** The set pieces were one per theme and two
  courses share a theme, so the sprint and the grand prix of each looked alike. The grand
  prix courses get their own: a windmill, a cider barn, a beached shipwreck, a terracotta
  arch, a mill wheel, a frozen waterfall, a dripstone hall, a mossy idol, a bone arch, a
  ring hung over the road, a banner gatehouse and a caged crystal.
- **Tidier courses.** Scenery, rails and pools stay off the racing line — no more cacti
  or camp fires in the road, or lava from a feature on a different part of the lap — and
  water and lava can no longer drain out over the island.
