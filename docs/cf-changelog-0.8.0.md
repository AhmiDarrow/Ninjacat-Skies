# 0.8.0 — Driftwrecks

- **Driftwrecks** (Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.1). After Soil is tensioned, pieces of the old world drift up to your Clowder's pad, hold for a while and unravel. Cross on a Tether Spool thread bridge, finish the wreck's objective, bring home Salvaged Weft, lore and Keepsakes.
- Modular: every wreck is assembled on arrival from a procedural keel and deck (six shapes), one of 54 core variants turned to any bearing, a scatter from 18 shared ruins and tethered islets — then one of nine Strand skins, six modifiers and five objectives (Salvage, Clear, Re-thread, Escort, Hold).
- Per-player chest rolls; anything left behind comes home in a Salvage Bundle. Nobody falls when a wreck unravels, and your own blocks stay.
- The **Wreck Atlas** (54 pages with Codex lore, perks for full rows and columns), **54 Keepsakes**, Tribe Banners and the Trophy Plinth.
- **Sealed rifts** in Hold wrecks with nine **Remnants**, and the one-time **Heartwreck**.
- New quest chapter, Codex category, `/driftwreck` admin commands and `driftwrecks-server.toml`.
