# 0.8.13 — The Songkeeper's Drum

Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.15, Tribal Power 5.3.6 and Chocobos Reborn 1.0.15. Existing saves load as they are,
no quest ids change, and the mod list is unchanged at 104.

- **A better crafting table (Craftweave, in Core).** When two recipes share a grid, a small button switches
  between them and remembers your pick: four string is white wool or two Void Yarn, your choice. Type how many
  you want under the result and one click makes them, refilling the grid from your pack. Star a recipe to keep
  it on a tab at the table's edge, and lay it out from your pack with a click. Works at the Tribal Bench too.
- **The Songkeeper Drum is a real rhythm game.** 46 songs, including the Ninjacat guardian themes and the
  Chocobo races, at four difficulties, with streaks, Spirit Surge, a Resonance meter, stars and boards. Put
  two drums side by side to duel a friend on the same song.
- **The Gate Drum's rite plays on the new drum game.** Same beat, same 60% to open the way to the March.
- **Spirit Charms cost what they are worth.** Every charm is a full recipe now, and the Sky Charm, which lets
  you fly, takes a Nether Star.
- **Machines show their real Pulse** in the Jade tooltip. **Camp decor hangs on fences, walls, chains and
  leaves.** A new Wind Charm with its own chimes, the **Hanging Rack**, the **recipe book at the Tribal Bench**,
  a tidier **Reagent Pouch**, and new sapling art.
- **Take cells and goggles back off gear** with an empty-cursor right-click in your inventory.
- **Chocobos run on long bloodlines.** A dash that runs the bar dry has to recover to 50; smart birds spend
  stamina more slowly and get more from boost pads; breeding climbs over many clutches toward rare legendary
  stats. Install the new Chocobos Reborn on the server and on every rider.

**Server owners:** unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again.
