# 0.8.9 — The March speaks

Tribal Power 5.0.1, Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.13 and Chocobos Reborn 1.0.13. Existing saves load as they are, no quest ids
change, and the mod list is unchanged at 104.

- **Tribal Power 4.1 to 5.0.1 — The world tells its own story.** Four releases in one, plus a recipe
  category fix in 5.0.1. The Elders
  talk: ask for work (three requests a day, a Tribe Mark every third) or for their story, a seven-step
  road that ends at a trial and a relic. Eight guardians keep the eight March countries, each rising
  from its own altar; all nine relics and a tablet of their cores perform the **Ninth Agreement**. The
  March has weather of its own (Ashfall, Glimmer Storm, Whiteout, Fen Mist), ley surges, festival
  days, wandering spirits and music, and calls storms two minutes ahead. The table: five March crops,
  the Hearth Pot, nine tribe dishes and six feasts; the Spirit Kettle brews tinctures, salves and
  incense; Sweat Stones and a Healing Circle mend you. Eight Spiritgear weapons, anointing at the Song
  Bench, the Pulse Crossbow, blessings and boons. The Chronicle: sixteen carved fragments of the Loom's
  history. And a long bug sweep — read Tribal Power's own notes for all of it.
- **Fifty new Tribal Weave quests and six Codex entries.** The chapter follows the new systems from
  the Hearth Pot and the Spirit Kettle to the eight guardians, the nine relics, the Ninth Agreement and
  the Chronicle; the Whisker Codex's Nine Tribes section explains Elders that talk, the guardians, the
  living March, the table, Spirit arms and which voice every device wants. No existing quest id
  changes.
- **Every automated hand answers to a voice.** Since Tribal Power 5.0 a Grove Tender, Wayanchor, Hush
  Totem, Summoning Cradle, Seal Loom, Tide Pump, Wind Snare or relay stands idle without a kept
  Resonance Totem of its voice within eight blocks, and says which one it wants. The Tribal Weave
  quests and the Codex say which voice each device needs; check your camp after updating.
- **Ready to translate.** Every line a player reads — chat, tooltips, signs, books and the whole
  Whisker Codex — now lives in lang files. If you would like the pack in your language, the files are
  on GitHub and translations are welcome.
- **Chocobos Reborn 1.0.13 — Clear pages.** The almanac's eight breeds are drawn at full detail
  again (since 1.0.10 they used the far-away model and looked crumpled).
- **A Dismount key.** Sneak no longer throws you out of the saddle, so it is free to steer: a
  chocobo descends on sneak without dropping you. Press **Caps Lock** (rebindable, under Ninjacat
  Skies in Controls) to get off anything you ride.
- **Your things wait in a Yarn Basket.** When you die, everything you drop, Curios included, goes
  into a basket of yarn balls where you fell, or where you last stood if you fell into the void.
  Right-click it to take it all back, or break it to spill it. Only you and your Clowder can.
- **Hall stall prices are real now.** A trade slot holds one stack, and the game quietly cut any
  price above 64 down to 64. Five Spark listings were meant to cost more, so the Nether Star was
  selling for 64 Thread instead of 1000. Big prices are now paid in **Thread Skeins** (9 Frayed
  Thread) and **Thread Bolts** (9 skeins, 81 Thread), which pack in a full crafting grid and unwind
  back at any time:
  - 2 Diamonds: 9 skeins (81 Thread)
  - 2 Item Vaults: 9 skeins (81 Thread)
  - Shulker Box: 22 skeins (198 Thread)
  - Thread Shard: 44 skeins (396 Thread)
  - Nether Star: 12 bolts (972 Thread)
- **Steward Caches are worth opening.** More of every roll is from the better pool: copper, iron
  and Thread in small caches; ingots, gold, redstone, lapis and experience in mediums; and diamonds,
  emeralds, ender pearls, obsidian or a Thread Skein in larges.
- **Animals, plants and decorations in every cache.** Caches now hold twice the parcels. Each one
  rolls the garden — saplings, seeds, crops, flowers, and sometimes a spawn egg (chicken, cow, pig
  and sheep most often, since a pad has no other way to get them; bigger caches add horses, bees,
  wolves, cats, turtles and more) — and a decoration: candles, lanterns, frames, carpets, banners.
- **A piece of a life in every seal.** Each cache also makes one rare draw: a **Thread Shard** at
  1.5% / 4% / 12% (small / medium / large), or a whole **Thread of Return** at 0.2% / 0.5% / 1.5%.
  Bigger seals always have the better odds, so combining caches never costs you a chance.

**Server owners:** nothing changes for you. The server zip still leaves out the four client-only
mods (Sodium, Reese's, BadOptimizations, Dynamic FPS); clients get all 104, servers 100. Unzip the
new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again.
