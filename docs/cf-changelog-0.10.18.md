# 0.10.18 — Every Piece Explains Itself

Tribal Power 5.6.2, Shamanic Mounts 0.1.14, Chocobos Reborn 1.1.13 and Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.22: a polish pass across all four. Nothing new to build, nothing moved. Existing saves load as they are. 106 mods.

**Across the pack**

- **Hover anything and it tells you what it is for.** Hundreds of items and blocks that said nothing now carry a grey hint line: what it does, what it needs, where it comes from. Machines, generators, totems, seals, lights, crops, dishes and feasts in Tribal Power; saddles, bags, eggs and the Trading Post in Shamanic Mounts; seeds, saddle, GP, gates and pads in Chocobos Reborn; the End Apple, Driftwrecks gear, Strand banners and Thread Meshes in the Core.
- **Reagents name themselves.** Every Tribal Power creature drop shows its note and the creature it comes from, what the note does when sung, and the anointment and remedy it brews into.
- **Tooltips wrap.** Long hint lines fold to tooltip width instead of running off the screen.
- **Mods list.** Each of the four mods now links to its CurseForge page and issue tracker and shows its logo.

**Fixes**

- Shamanic Mounts: the Mount Trading Post is now mined with a pickaxe at the right speed instead of breaking at hand speed.
