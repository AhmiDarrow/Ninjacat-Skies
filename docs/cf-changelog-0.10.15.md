# 0.10.15 — The Ley Heart

Tribal Power 5.5.0. Everything else stays as it is in 0.10.14. Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 106 mods.

**Tribal Power**

- **The Ley Heart.** A six-totem multiblock on a dais of Anchor Stones that burns crystals, reagents and water for about 1,100 to 4,500 Pulse a second, and raises six real ley lines out through its totems.
- **Reagent Threads.** Reagents from strong-blooded creatures and elites carry a Thread from I to III, the Ley Heart's best fuel.
- **Spiritgear spends Pulse, not its edge.** Paid actions take no wear, tools last their true 1,561 uses, and every piece mends with Manifested Ingots on any anvil.
- **Verse arrows carry their verse**, up to 3 power from copies of the lead reagent.
- **Fairer blessings and armour.** The Loom blessing returns its full share, and Spiritweave switched Off or dry stops every perk.
- **The Hearth Pot reads its square.** Each ingredient goes in the seat the recipe shows. New: Fish and Chips, the most filling meal in camp.
- **Water creatures swim** and now really spawn.
- **Tide Pump** draws water from below and works with fluid relay plates; relay plates work on every camp device that holds goods, and camp devices work on vanilla mobs and crops too.
- **Builder's Chalk laser level**: a straight line 64, 128 or 256 blocks out.
- **Four creative tabs** so JEI and EMI list the mod in order, and new Codex chapters.
