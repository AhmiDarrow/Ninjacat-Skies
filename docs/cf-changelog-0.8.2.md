# 0.8.2 — Plain sight

New builds of the pack's three own mods: a bug sweep, one art pass across all of them, and a way into the End. Existing saves load as they are and no quest ids change.

- **A way to the End (Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.3, file 8925254).** Twelve portal frames are not a pad craft, so the End had no route. The new **End Apple** costs five ender pearls, two bones and two blaze powder: the first bite puts you on the End's obsidian pad and leaves a bitten half, and eating that brings you back to the spot you ate from. The way back is stored on you, so dying in the End does not lose it. New quest in the End chapter.
- **One look across the pack.** Everything is drawn at 32x32 to one style now. Driftwrecks' 127 items and blocks are redrawn, its 54 Keepsakes are 54 different objects, and Tribe Banners, Keepsakes, Strand Tokens and Post notches all carry the same nine tribe marks as Tribal Power. Voidloom's placeholder scraps and identical meshes are real sprites. Guardians' relics and totems are redrawn, and the bosses and Remnants wear pixel-quantised textures instead of smooth 3D bakes. Tribal Power's creature drops, grits, exotic wood sets and machine tops are their own objects. Chocobos Reborn's greens, nuts, armour, tack and crop stages are hand-drawn.
- **Core fixes.** The idol's pillar order and the Remnant cues play in order again, so those puzzles can be read. Boss fights survive a server restart. Blocks you place on an arena stage drop when broken. The Thread Lock opens fully, the Remnant boss bar keeps its Strand after a reload, and `sunderedSky = false` restores the sunrise.
- **Tribal Power 3.6.2 - Plain sight (file 8925225).** Gates no longer bounce you back and forth for Pulse. Familiars keep their health through a relog. Spiritgear swings belong to the player who made them. Machines cost far less each tick, tanks no longer drain through their faces, and logic plates keep their memory.
- **Chocobos Reborn 1.0.7 - Fresh paint (file 8925242).** Mud only slows birds on a race course, not in every swamp. The bookie quotes the heat's real odds, scratching no longer sends you home, and crowds from crashed heats are cleared.
- **Quests and text.** Unfinishable quests fixed (Spirit Fire, which gated six others, Pam's Apple, the Square Gate). The retired Desk is gone from five quests, the Nether chapter names routes that exist in a void Nether, and the text matches Tribal Power 3.6.2 and Chocobos Reborn 1.0.7. Voidloom meshes and March soil get the pack's extra sieve drops.
- **Setup.** The shipped Core config is complete, so NeoForge no longer rewrites it and leaves a `.bak` on a fresh install. Silent Gear's "open item" moves to **Alt+O**; Alt+X was also the backpack's second upgrade toggle.
