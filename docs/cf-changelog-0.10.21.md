# 0.10.21 — Rod, Shard and a Meal

Tribal Power 5.6.5 and Shamanic Mounts 0.1.16: three things players asked for. Nothing new to build, nothing moved. Existing saves load as they are. 106 mods.

**Tribal Power**

- **Fish the March.** A fishing rod cast in any March water pulls up Raw Glimmerfin in place of cod and salmon, and one fish catch in four is a Raw Silt Eel. Junk and treasure come up as before. Hold an empty bucket in your other hand and a glimmerfin lands alive as a Bucket of Glimmerfin, ready to tip into a pond and breed with Ribbon Weed or seagrass.
- **Amethyst shatters.** The Echo Shatter breaks an amethyst block back into four Amethyst Shards: Earth voice, four seconds, twenty Pulse a second.

**Shamanic Mounts**

- **Meat mends a hurt mount.** Right-click a hurt tame with any meat, raw or cooked, from any mod. It eats one and heals by what the cut is worth, never past full. A sound mount ignores the meat and the click still rides. Wild mounts never take it, so food cannot tame anything.
