# 0.10.4 — Mended and Measured, Earned in Class and Drums Side by Side

Tribal Power 5.3.13 and Chocobos Reborn 1.1.4, with Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.20 and Shamanic Mounts 0.1.4 as before.
Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 105 mods. Clients and servers must update together.

**Tribal Power**

- **Exploits closed.** Items taken by hand from lattice machines, relays, horns and benches no longer come back after a
  reload, a Song Bench result that does not fit drops at your feet, silk-touched ores pay their XP once, and one marked
  wall no longer answers every building request a tribe asks for.
- **What's yours stays yours.** Strangers can no longer re-link or cut your gate keystones, re-bind your Wireless Relay,
  or brush your bonded animals for the double reagent.
- **Machines tell the truth.** Comparators hear a stalled Stone Font or Resonance Mesh and a fading Silent Drum, the Wind
  Snare and Ward Drum stop wasting Pulse, and the Ley Collector and Pulse Gauge read right. A Kinship Totem counts for
  the voice gate wherever a kept Resonance Totem does.
- **The Grove Tender fells trees, not houses.** Only grown trees, felled to the ground; log builds are left alone.
- **Boss themes play**, familiars and guardian altars behave after revives, unloads and naps, and drum practice no
  longer locks you out until you relog.
- **Screens and the Codex.** Recipe labels, captions, Elder dialogue and familiar names fit their panels, and about sixty
  item names in the Codex now link. The server does less work every tick in big bases.

**Chocobos**

- **The farm line is earned in the right class.** Colour breeding now counts only first places won in that stage's
  class. Green or Blue: two Good Yellows with 5 Class C wins each (certain at 16 combined). Black: a Great Green and a
  Great Blue with 8 Class B wins each (certain at 24). Gold: a Wonderful Yellow and a Wonderful Black with 10 Class A
  wins each (certain at 32). Birds you already have get their wins shared out by class once, and the almanac shows
  first places by class.
- **Ahmi and Risika ride Class C.** Ranked Class C heats seat them in the named stalls, on Yellows in Flame and Purple
  plumage, with Risika a touch ahead; Teiyo and Jolo take over from Class B.
- **A longer climb per class.** Promotion bars are now 36 points for Class C, 54 for Class B and 72 for Class A. Birds
  you already have scale onto the new bars when the world loads, and every sign, board and the almanac say so.

**Loom's End**

- **Duel at the Drum Circle.** Its two Songkeeper Drums now stand side by side, so two players at them can duel.
  Existing worlds rebuild the hub once.

**Server owners:** unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again.
