# Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.14

**Craftweave: a better crafting table.** Existing saves load as they are; no ids change.

Craftweave is a new part of Core. It works at the vanilla crafting table and at any 3x3 table with a recipe book, the Tribal Bench included, and it only shows something when it has a job to do.

**Recipes that share a grid**

- **When more than one recipe fits the grid, a small "2/3" button appears above the result.** Click it for the next recipe, right-click for the previous. Four string in a square, for example, is both white wool and two Void Yarn: pick the one you want.
- **Your pick is remembered.** The next time those recipes meet on a grid, the table offers the one you chose last.

**Craft as many as you need**

- **A small box under the result takes a number.** Type 64 (or scroll over it, with shift for a stack at a time), then click the result or press Enter, and the table makes that many straight into your pack.
- **When the grid runs out, it is laid out again from your pack** with the same recipe, until you have what you asked for or your pack runs dry.
- **"max N" beside the box** says how many you can make from what you carry. Empty or 1 is the table as it always was, and shift-click still crafts a full stack.

**Bookmarks**

- **The star beside the result pins its recipe.** Pinned recipes wait on a slim tab at the table's right edge that opens when you hover it.
- **Click a bookmark to lay the recipe out from your pack** (shift for as many as fit), even if the recipe book has not unlocked it yet. The tooltip says how many you can make right now. Right-click a bookmark to drop it.

**Clear the grid**

- **A small x by the grid puts everything on it back in your pack.**
