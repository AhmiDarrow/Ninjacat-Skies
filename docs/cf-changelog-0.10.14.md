# 0.10.14 — The March Remade

Tribal Power 5.4.0. Everything else stays as it is in 0.10.13.
Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 106 mods.

**Tribal Power**

- **Every creature remade.** All 97 creatures have new models and skins, each shaped like the animal its name promises,
  and the guardians are rebuilt from the ground up. Overlapping faces that flickered at a distance are gone.
- **A true bestiary.** The Spirit Codex draws each creature from its own live model, turning slowly; drag it to turn it
  yourself. Every creature has its own page, with where it lives, what it drops and what it eats.
- **The Codex, checked against the game.** Around two hundred pages corrected or filled in, and core pages that were
  hidden behind the spoiler switch are open again.
- **Relay channels and void plates.** Ranked plates open up to four channels, each with its own destination and filter,
  sent in priority order or in turn. A Fire Seal plate burns what its filter allows.
- **Plain clicks.** Relay plates go onto machines, the Lattice Tuner marks and aims, and Ritual Chalk links totems, all
  without crouching. The Stone Font takes plates on its top and bottom.
- **Two-block totems, the Gathering Charm and the Earth axe.** Resonance Totems stand two blocks tall; a new Air charm
  draws items and experience to you; an Earth Spiritgear axe fells the whole tree.
- **Familiars, game and herds.** Attune a familiar at a totem to strengthen that voice's magic. Gentle creatures drop
  Raw Game and March Leather and can all be bred.
- **More Glimmer Ridge**, and its grazers can now rise there.
