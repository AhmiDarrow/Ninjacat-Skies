# 0.10.0 — Loom's End

Clowder Hall is rebuilt as **Loom's End**, the last knot at the edge of the cut world, raised by the nine tribes and
every craft of the sky. Core 0.5.18. Existing saves rebuild the hub once on the first visit after updating; your
things in the Hall (the drop chest and any Yarn Basket) are left exactly as they were. No quest ids change. 105 mods.

- **Loom's End.** The Knot with its nine strands and the ceremony pad at the heart, the Cut Loom towering over it with
  its warp severed mid-air, and the broken gate-path beyond. Every quarter is built by a tribe in its own wood, stone,
  glass and colour: tribe homes, the Purring Hearth inn, the Stitchers' Chapel, the Leaf Archive, the Pattern-weavers'
  clock tower, the Artificers' Yard, the Old Grove, an apiary, a paddock, a garden, the Drum Circle, and the Windward
  Mill on its own islet across a rope bridge. Blocks from across the pack: Tribal Power, Driftwrecks, Voidloom,
  Guardians, Chocobos Reborn, Shamanic Mounts, Create, Farmer's Delight, Handcrafted, Macaw's Roofs and Bridges,
  Supplementaries, Ars Nouveau, Occultism, Nature's Aura, Iron's Spellbooks, AE2, Mekanism, Powah, Productive Bees,
  Pam's, Mystical Agriculture, Ex Deorum, Silent Gear, Botany Pots, Comforts and more.
- **Ten keepers, ten shops.** Each tribe keeps a real shop with a counter and its wares on the walls: Pad-keepers,
  Rootbinders, Grit-singers, Pattern-weavers, Colony-keepers, Loom-stitchers, Drumhearts, Edge-walkers, Seal-carvers
  and the Hearth-keeper. Stock moved to its rightful keeper and 55 new goods were added (seeds and saplings, honey and
  wax, yarn and dyes, pages and paper, rope and road gear, clocks and charts, hearth food).
- **Saddles.** The Pad-keepers sell a saddle for one block of diamond.
- **A town that lives.** Townsfolk with their own beds and days, tribe kin at their work (two Drumheart drummers on
  the Drum Circle stage), cats, sheep in the nine tribe colours, cows, pigs, hens, bees, parrots, the spirit herd,
  town chocobos, and **Sunfeather**, the Hall's golden chocobo. None of them can be tamed, led, ridden or traded.
- **The Drum Circle.** Two Songkeeper Drums side by side: stand at one to play alone, or at both to duel.
- **The Hall keeps time.** It follows the world's day now, so the town works, gathers at the bell and sleeps.
- **Nothing in the Hall is taken or broken.** Only the drop chest, Yarn Baskets, doors, the drums, the bell and craft
  tables respond; barrels, crates, crops, hives and the rest stay as the town left them.

**Server owners:** unzip the new server pack over the old folder and run `install` again. Keep `spawn-npcs` and
`spawn-animals` on (the defaults) or the townsfolk and animals are removed by the game.
