# 0.10.6 — Hearth and Wire, Close at Heel, Lighter Everywhere

Tribal Power 5.3.15, Chocobos Reborn 1.1.5, Shamanic Mounts 0.1.7 and Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.21.
Existing saves load as they are; no quest ids change. 105 mods. Clients and servers must update together.

**Tribal Power**

- **Fourteen new Hearth Pot meals** from March and ordinary ingredients, each with its own use: road food, a camp
  antidote, dishes for climbing, diving, mining, the dark and hard nights.
- **Wireless song plates.** Sync plates with the Totem Wrench instead of laying thread; plates now belong to whoever
  set them down and their camp.
- **The Pulse Bow and Crossbow** are made from Manifested Ingots, mend at the Spirit Anvil and hit like a fair step up
  from vanilla instead of tap-firing for near-full damage. The crossbow is held the right way round.
- **Eels you can find.** March swimmers now come to open water, not water sealed under the land, and Silt Eels count as
  fish, so the Reed Fen and the Shallows have them again.
- **Spawn eggs you can tell apart**, and a Spirit Codex that reads cleanly at the default size of a 1080p screen, with
  real Pulse costs, better search and corrected facts.

**Shamanic Mounts**

- **Mounts follow by default** and keep following when you step off; following mounts come with you through portals
  and teleports. Tell a mount to stay and it stays.
- **About half the drawing cost** per mount, and fliers no longer hitch as they take off.

**Chocobos and the hub**

- **Resting chocobos and Square stewards** cost far less on the server, and birds draw about twice as fast.
- **Loom's End** stops running mob spawning it could never use (about a fifth of its tick), and its residents' rescue
  sweep works: Sunfeather and anyone who falls are brought home again.
