# Shared Clowder lives

Lives are enabled by default. Each FTB team has one persistent pool, with each member contributing three starting lives: one member starts at 3, two at 6, three at 9. Offline members count. New members add their contribution once; reconnecting or leaving and rejoining the same team never refills it. Survival/adventure deaths spend one life. Creative and spectator players do not spend lives. At zero, surviving members become spectators and the dead member becomes a spectator on respawn. Offline members are checked when they return.

`/skybound lives` reports the current pool. Six scattered progression milestones offer a **Thread of Return**, each adding exactly one shared life: Seat Clock, Seat Sigil, Dragon Egg Show, Ultimate Cube, Thirteen voices, one sky, and Reweave. Claiming a reward credits the pool directly. Each milestone is recorded once per team, including across reloads and operator revives. Team size does not multiply them.

Past those six the pack is long enough to need a repeatable life, so there is one priced path and no other. `ninjacatskies:thread_of_return` is an item: right-click it for exactly one shared life. It keeps **no receipt**, so a Clowder can earn it as often as it can pay — the price is the limit, not a counter. It is built from four `ninjacatskies:thread_shard` around a Braid Cord — the Cord is spun by right-clicking a Tension Post with a Strand Filament, which needs nothing seated, so the recipe is readable from the moment a Clowder raises a Post — and a Shard is either crafted (four diamonds, four Void Yarn, one `driftwrecks:rift_shard` from a mended Remnant) or bought from the Spark stall at `/clowder hub` for 400 Frayed Thread, four per Minecraft day. One life is therefore four Remnant kills or 1600 Thread. There are still no mob-drop, loot-crate or free repeatable life rewards, and nothing grants more than one life at a time.

A Clowder at zero is already spectating and cannot use an item, so a Thread of Return only helps if it is held **before** the pool empties. That is deliberate: it is insurance, not a resurrection. At zero with nothing earned left, an operator must still decide whether to revive the team.

Frayed Thread itself can be earned back across the **Grit** counter at `/clowder hub`, which takes blocks of copper, iron, gold, amethyst, emerald and diamond, and netherite ingots, and pays Thread for them. The rates are 9–15× worse than buying the same material back from a stall (a diamond block returns 24 Thread; Spark sells two diamonds for 80), so no exchange loop pays, and stall stock restocks once per Minecraft day. See `pack/overrides/kubejs/data/ninjacatskies/tribalpower/dock_shop/grit_exchange_*.json`.

Operator-only `/skybound revive [player]` restores that player's team to the configured starting count multiplied by current team membership. `/clowder revive` is also operator-only. Ordinary players cannot reset their pool with a command.

Configure `hardcore.livesEnabled` and `hardcore.startingLives` in `config/ninjacatskies-common.toml`. With lives switched off a Thread of Return refuses to be spent rather than being wasted. FTB team membership determines the pool; changing teams switches to the destination team's pool.
